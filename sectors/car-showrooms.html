Car showroom and dealership tiling

Big, flat, showroom floors that show off the cars, laid with no lipping.

Overview

A showroom floor is part of the display. Large, often polished porcelain tiles need a very flat base and careful laying, or every lip and uneven joint shows under the lights.

We tile showroom floors, receptions, customer lounges, washrooms and service reception areas in car showrooms and dealerships.

What we tile

  • Large-format showroom floors
  • Receptions and customer lounges
  • Customer and staff washrooms
  • Service reception and handover areas
  • Repairs to damaged showroom tiles

What matters

A flat base

Large-format tiles show every high and low spot, so floors are checked and levelled before tiling.

No lipping

Levelling systems and full adhesive coverage keep large tiles flat and solid.

Weight of vehicles

Showroom floors carry cars, so the base, adhesive and tile must all be suitable for the load.

Protecting the finish

Polished tiles are protected after laying so they hand over looking perfect.

How we work on site

Common questions

Can you lay large-format tiles like 1200 x 600 mm or bigger?

Yes. We regularly lay large-format porcelain and slabs. The key is a flat base and the right adhesive.

Can you replace a cracked showroom tile?

Yes. A damaged tile can be cut out and replaced, ideally with spares from the original job.

Can you work around opening hours?

Yes. We can work in sections, out of hours or on closed days.
