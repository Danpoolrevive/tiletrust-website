Overview

Residential developments need consistency: every bathroom, kitchen and hallway to the same specification, finished on programme so the next trade can follow on.

We tile bathrooms, en-suites, kitchens and communal areas in apartment blocks, new-build developments, student accommodation, retirement living and housing association homes.

What we tile

  • Bathrooms, en-suites and wet rooms
  • Kitchen floors and splashbacks
  • Communal entrance halls and corridors
  • Stairwells and lift lobbies
  • Show homes and show apartments
  • Snagging and repairs

What matters

Consistent quality

The same setting out, joint widths and finish in every unit.

On programme

We plan around the build programme and other trades so units are handed over on time.

Waterproofing

Bathrooms and wet rooms are tanked properly before tiling.

Clean handover

Each unit is left clean and ready for the next trade or the new owner.

How we work on site

  • We keep dust down with vacuums and dust extractors and clean up as we go.
  • We protect finished work and surrounding areas, and leave the site clean and tidy every day.
  • We work evenings, nights and weekends when a programme or opening hours call for it.
  • Both of us are enhanced DBS checked, hold CSCS cards and are trained in COSHH, manual handling, abrasive wheels and asbestos and silica awareness.
  • Risk assessments, method statements and COSHH information are available on request.

Common questions

Do you work for main contractors and developers?

Yes. We work as the tiling contractor for builders, developers and housing providers.

Can you supply the tiles and materials?

Yes, through specialist UK suppliers, or we can fit materials supplied by you.

Can you tile communal areas in occupied buildings?

Yes. We phase the work, keep routes safe and clean up properly each day.
