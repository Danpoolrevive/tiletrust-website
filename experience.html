Our experience

Experience you can trust.

Three generations of tilers. Over 40 years on the tools. Projects of every size, all over the UK.

Request a quoteCall 07434 005958

A trade passed down, a standard kept.

Tiling has run through our family for three generations. Jimmy taught his son Jay, who has now been tiling for 44 years. Jay brought his son Dan onto the tools in 2007.

Between us we have worked on almost every kind of tiling there is, and every job has taught us something we carry into the next one.

Where we have worked

From landmark projects to the smallest detail.

Over the years we have worked on some of the busiest, most demanding buildings in the country. Large or small, every job gets the same care.

Sport and leisure

Training facilities for a Premier League football club, and leisure centres.

Transport

An international airport, and train stations.

Retail and city centres

A major city-centre shopping destination, supermarkets, car showrooms and shopfitting.

Workplaces

Office blocks, cyber security offices, the Leeds headquarters of a national betting brand, and factories.

Education and living

Universities and apartment developments.

Further afield

Farms, and the outsides of buildings in city centres.

Relationships built to last.

Most of our work comes from people who have worked with us before. Main contractors, facilities managers and clients know we will turn up when we say, keep the site tidy, and solve problems before they become theirs.

We go above and beyond on every job, because the next project depends on how we finish this one.

A small team, with a trusted network behind it.

TileTrust is a family business, and we like it that way. You deal directly with Dan and Jay, from the first call to the final clean-down, and one of us is on every job. When a project needs more hands, we bring in tradesmen we have worked alongside for years and trust to tile to our standard.

Always honest about our diary.

If we are fully booked, we will tell you straight away and give you a realistic start date. If your timescale can't wait, we will try to recommend a good tradesman we know and trust. If we can help, we will, so it's always worth getting in touch.
