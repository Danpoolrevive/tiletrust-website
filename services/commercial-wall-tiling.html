What to know

Check the background

Plaster, plasterboard, blockwork and tile backer boards all behave differently. Each has a weight limit for tiles and adhesive, so heavy tiles may need a different background.

Weight limits

As a guide, gypsum plaster typically takes up to about 20kg per square metre of tile and adhesive, and unskimmed plasterboard about 32kg. Heavier tiles may need cement board or a different background.

Wet areas need tanking

Showers and changing rooms need a waterproof layer behind the tiles. Grout and tiles alone are not waterproof.

Common questions

Can you tile over existing tiles?

Sometimes, if the old tiles are sound and well bonded and the extra weight is within limits. Often it is better to strip back and start clean, and we'll tell you which applies.

Do you do bathrooms?

Yes. Alongside our commercial work we tile bathrooms, en-suites, walk-in showers and wet rooms. See our bathroom and wet room tiling page for more.

Can you tile over existing wall tiles?

Sometimes, if the old tiles are firmly bonded and the wall can take the extra weight. Often it's better to remove them. We'll check before quoting.

Do walls need tanking?

Walls in showers and wet areas should be waterproofed before tiling. Dry areas usually just need a sound, primed surface.
