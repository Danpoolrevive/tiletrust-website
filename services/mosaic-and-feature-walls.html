Overview

Mosaics and feature walls make receptions, spas, restaurants and leisure areas stand out. We fit glass and ceramic mosaics, patterns and feature tiling with clean, consistent joints.

  • Logos and patterns set out from drawings

What to know

Set-out

A good mosaic or feature wall depends on careful set-out, so cuts are balanced and patterns line up.

Adhesive and grout

Glass mosaic often needs a white adhesive so the colour isn't spoiled, and the grout colour changes the whole look.

Pools

Pool mosaics are handled through our specialist pool division, PoolRevive.

How we work on site

Mosaics show every uneven joint, so we check walls are flat, set out the pattern carefully and keep joints consistent across the sheets.

  • We keep dust down with vacuums and dust extractors and clean up as we go.
  • We protect finished work and surrounding areas, and leave the site clean and tidy every day.
  • We work evenings, nights and weekends when a programme or opening hours call for it.
  • Both of us are enhanced DBS checked, hold CSCS cards and are trained in COSHH, manual handling, abrasive wheels and asbestos and silica awareness.

Common questions

Can you fit a logo in mosaic?

Yes, from supplied artwork or drawings, set out before fixing.

Are mosaics suitable for floors?

Yes, with the right tile and slip rating. Many mosaics are designed for wet floors and pools.

Can mosaics go in showers and pools?

Yes. Glass and porcelain mosaics suit wet areas well, laid on a waterproofed background with a suitable grout.

How do you avoid visible sheet lines?

By keeping joints consistent between sheets and setting out the wall before starting, so the pattern runs evenly.
