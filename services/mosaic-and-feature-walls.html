Overview

Mosaics and feature walls make receptions, spas, restaurants and leisure areas stand out. We fit glass and ceramic mosaics, patterns and feature tiling with clean, consistent joints.

What we do

  • Glass and ceramic mosaics
  • Feature walls in receptions, bars and restaurants
  • Spas, leisure areas and pools
  • Curved surfaces and columns
  • Logos and patterns set out from drawings

What to know

Set-out

A good mosaic or feature wall depends on careful set-out, so cuts are balanced and patterns line up.

Adhesive and grout

Glass mosaic often needs a white adhesive so the colour isn't spoiled, and the grout colour changes the whole look.

Pools

Pool mosaics are handled through our specialist pool division, PoolRevive.

Common questions

Can you fit a logo in mosaic?

Yes, from supplied artwork or drawings, set out before fixing.

Are mosaics suitable for floors?

Yes, with the right tile and slip rating. Many mosaics are designed for wet floors and pools.
