Large-format porcelain tiling

Big tiles and slabs fitted flat, with no lipping.

Overview

Large-format porcelain gives a clean, modern finish with fewer grout lines, but it is far less forgiving than standard tiles. We prepare the base, bed every tile fully, and use levelling systems to keep the surface flat.

What we do

  • Large porcelain tiles and slabs for floors and walls
  • Floor flattening with self-levelling compounds
  • Full-bed adhesive coverage and back-buttering
  • Tile levelling systems to control lipping
  • Lifting equipment and handling frames for big slabs
  • Receptions, showrooms, retail and hospitality areas

What to know

Flatness matters

Big tiles show any unevenness in the floor as lipping between tiles. A flat base is the most important part of the job.

Full adhesive coverage

Large tiles need a solid bed of adhesive with no voids, which is why they are usually back-buttered as well as bedded.

Bowing and offset

Long rectangular tiles can have a slight bow. Limiting the offset, often to a third of the tile length or less, helps avoid lipping.

How we work on site

With large-format tiles we make sure the base is flat, use the right adhesive for full coverage and use levelling systems to keep the tiles flat and lip-free.

Common questions

Are large-format tiles harder to fit?

Yes. They need a flatter base, more adhesive and careful handling, so they usually cost more to install than standard tiles.

Can large-format tiles go on walls?

Yes, provided the background can take the weight. We check this before we start.

Do large tiles need a special adhesive?

Large-format tiles usually need a flexible adhesive with full coverage on the back of the tile and the floor, so there are no hollow spots.

Can large-format tiles go on walls?

Yes, as long as the wall is flat, sound and strong enough for the weight. We'll check before we start.
