Services

Tiling services, from commercial contracts to bathrooms

If it can be tiled, we can tile it. From floor preparation to the final joint, carried out by the same experienced tilers from start to finish.

Request a quoteCall 07434 005958

Wall & floor tiling

  • Ceramic and porcelainWall and floor tiles in all sizes and finishes.
  • Large-format porcelainBig tiles and slabs that need a flat base and careful handling to avoid lipping.
  • MosaicGlass and ceramic mosaics for feature walls, wet areas and pools.
  • Natural stoneMarble, limestone, slate and granite.
  • Quarry tilesTraditional hard-wearing floors for kitchens and back-of-house areas.
  • Feature wallsReceptions, hospitality spaces and statement areas.

Floor preparation

  • Self-levelling compoundsA flat, smooth base over uneven concrete and existing floors.
  • Priming and surface prepMaking sure the base is sound, clean and ready to bond.
  • Uncoupling membranesHelp protect tiles from movement and cracking in the base below.
  • Underfloor heatingTiling over wet and electric underfloor heating systems.

Wet areas & safety floors

  • Tanking and waterproofingBefore tiling showers, changing rooms and wet areas.
  • Changing rooms and showersFor sports grounds, schools, leisure centres and gyms.
  • Anti-slip floorsSlip-resistant tiles chosen for how the area is used, wet or dry.
  • Epoxy groutingStain- and chemical-resistant joints for kitchens, wet areas and pools.
  • Drainage and channelsFloors laid to falls with channel drains and gratings.

Repairs, maintenance & supply

  • Tile repairsReplacing cracked, loose or damaged tiles.
  • Commercial regroutingRaking out and regrouting to extend the life of tiling.
  • Pool cleansProfessional pool tile cleans through PoolRevive.
  • Supply and fitTiles, adhesives, grouts, levelling compounds, primers, membranes and tanking through specialist UK suppliers.
  • Fit onlyFitting tiles supplied by the client or main contractor.
  • Access and equipmentScaffolding and specialist equipment hire for work at height.

What we take on: large commercial contracts, public buildings and quality domestic work, including bathrooms, en-suites and wet rooms.
