Swimming pools: PoolRevive, our pool division

Swimming pool tiling, repairs and regrouting are handled by PoolRevive, the specialist pool division of TileTrust Ltd.

Visit poolrevive.co.ukCall 07434 005958

PoolRevive is the name we use for all of our swimming pool work: pool tiling, chemical cleans, tile repairs, epoxy regrouting and full refurbishment for leisure centres, schools, hotels, health clubs and private pools. It is carried out by the same tilers who run TileTrust, with the same standards on every job.

For pool enquiries, photos and full details of each service, please visit the PoolRevive website. TileTrust continues to handle commercial floor and wall tiling, including the changing rooms, showers and wet areas around a pool, and the wider gym and leisure centre spaces beyond it.
