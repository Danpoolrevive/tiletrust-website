Pool services

Complete pool tiling care.

From a one-day clean to a full rip-out and retile, carried out by tilers who've spent years in pools.

Pool tiling

Mosaic, step treads, edge tiles, corner pieces and drainage tiles.

Pool cleans

Professional chemical cleans to remove calcium, rust marks and staining.

Tile repairs

Replacing loose, cracked and missing tiles, matched as closely as possible.

Epoxy regrouting

Long-lasting, chemical-resistant grout, left 7 days to cure before refilling.

Full refurbishment

Rip-out, re-render, retile and regrout.

Edges, channels and covers

Edge tiles, channel grating and tiled-in or plastic access covers.
